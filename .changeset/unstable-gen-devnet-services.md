`@polyester/sdk/unstable/gen` adds `assistantPb` (the AI assistant), `priceAlertsPb` (price alerts), `systemPb` (`getVersion`) and `quickswapReferralPb` (QuickSwap referrals). `quickswapBrokerPb` adds broker payouts and vault payout rules, and `apiKeysPb` adds the assistant key methods. The assistant, price alerts and assistant keys are available on devnet only.
